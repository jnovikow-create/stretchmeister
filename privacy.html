Stretchmeister Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.Stretchmeister

Short version: Your plan, check-ins, and streak stay on your iPhone. There is no account. A Google banner can sit above the tabs. Ad requests are non-personalized.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves your training plan on the device:

  • Sport, position, minutes, goals, and areas you marked tight or injured
  • Daily check-ins for energy, sleep, and soreness
  • Finished sessions, XP, badges, and the streak

Justin Novikow does not receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes this save unless it is in a device backup.

2. Advertising

A Google AdMob banner can appear above the tabs. It stays off during a timed stretch and during the setup survey. Each request is non-personalized. The app does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps. Google may process advertising data and a device identifier to fill and measure the banner. Your sport, check-ins, and streak stay on the phone. Google’s privacy policy.

3. What stays on the device

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps
  • No sale of your plan
  • No analytics of your stretches
  • The routine itself is stored inside the app. The banner loads from Google.

4. Health

Stretchmeister is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis. The words you enter about tight spots or injuries are stored only on the device so the routine can avoid harder holds.
