Stretchmeister Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.Stretchmeister

Short version: Stretchmeister does not collect data. Your plan, check-ins, and streak stay on your iPhone. There is no account and no advertising.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves your training plan on the device:

  • Sport, position, minutes, goals, and areas you marked tight or injured
  • Daily check-ins for energy, sleep, and soreness
  • Finished sessions, XP, badges, and the streak

Justin Novikow does not receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes this save unless it is in a device backup.

2. What this app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No advertising and no tracking
  • No sale of information
  • No analytics service
  • No network requests. The routine is stored inside the app.

3. Health

Stretchmeister is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis. The words you enter about tight spots or injuries are stored only on the device so the routine can avoid harder holds.
