How a routine works

  • The first launch asks for your sport, position, tight spots, minutes, and goals.
  • Today shows the stretches, the hold time, and a streak count.
  • Start routine runs a timer for each hold. Two-sided stretches ask for the other side.
  • Check-in records energy, sleep, and sore spots. A low day shortens the harder holds.
  • Progress shows XP, badges, and the days you finished.
  • The plan asks for an update about every two weeks.

Safety

Stretchmeister is general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice, and it does not diagnose an injury. Stretch only to a mild pull. Stop if a movement pinches, is sharp, or makes a symptom worse. Ask a qualified professional about pain or an injury.

Common questions

Where is my streak?
On this iPhone. Deleting the app removes it. A device backup can restore it if Stretchmeister was included.

Is there an account?
No. There are no ads and no sign-in.
